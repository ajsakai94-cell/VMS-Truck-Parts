Spin-on air dryer cartridge with oil separator for the air processing unit on the MP4 Mercedes-Benz Actros. Keeps water and oil out of the brake air system. Change it every 1 to 2 years, or sooner if you're draining water from the tanks. Check your old cartridge has the M39 x 1.5 thread before you order, or send us your VIN and we'll confirm fit. New stock is on its way.

At a glance:

  • Thread: M39 x 1.5
  • Size: about 165 mm tall, 136 mm across
  • Type: Spin-on desiccant cartridge with oil separator
  • Change: Every 1 to 2 years, or when water shows in the air tanks
