Rear Brake Pad Set (Axle Set) to suit Mercedes-Benz Actros MP4 / MP5

Rear axle brake pad set for the MP4 Mercedes-Benz Actros with Knorr-Bremse SL7 disc brakes. 4 pads, enough to do both wheels on one rear axle (order 2 sets for a tandem drive). Front and rear pads are different sizes, so check the WVA number on your old pad (29244 or 29245) before you order, or send us your VIN and we'll confirm fit. If your old pads say WVA 29315 you have Wabco MAXX22L calipers and these won't fit. New stock is on its way.

At a glance:

  • Axle: Rear (drive axle)
  • Caliper: Knorr-Bremse SL7
  • WVA: 29244 / 29245
  • Pad size: 207 x 113 x 30 mm
  • In the box: 4 pads, enough for one rear axle (both wheels)
