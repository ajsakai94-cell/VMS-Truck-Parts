7 Pin Trailer Suzi Coil – Lighting Cable to suit Mercedes-Benz Actros MP4 / MP5

Part no. VMS-B06

7 pin coiled suzi that carries the trailer light circuits (tail, stop, indicators and auxiliary) from the prime mover to the trailer. Metal-bodied plugs with a spring guard to stop the cable kinking at the plug. An alternative to Narva, Hella and Trail Link 7 core trailer suzi coils. For the trailer brakes' ABS/EBS connection use our ABS/EBS suzi coil. Check the plug on your truck and trailer matches before you order, or call us and we'll confirm.

At a glance:

  • Plugs: 7-pin, both ends
  • Use: Trailer lights (not for ABS/EBS)
  • Type: Coiled suzi with metal-bodied plugs and spring guard
