Injector Line Seal (OM471 / OM473) to suit Mercedes-Benz Actros MP4 / MP5

OE A 472 078 04 80 · Part no. VMS-E08

Aftermarket fuel injector line seal to suit the OM471 and OM473 engines in the MP4 / MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros, OE A 472 078 04 80 (also listed as A 472 078 01 80). It seals the injector line where it passes through the valve cover, so a hard or split one lets oil weep onto the lines and down the side of the head. There's one per cylinder, six per engine, and it's worth replacing them whenever the valve cover comes off or the injectors are done. Fit it with TOP facing up. Priced per seal.

At a glance:

  • Position: Valve cover, where the fuel injector line passes through (1 per cylinder)
  • Per engine: 6
  • Type: Rubber seal bonded to a two-bolt metal flange, marked TOP
  • Engines: OM471 12.8 L, OM473 15.6 L
