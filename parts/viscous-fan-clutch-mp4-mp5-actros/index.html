Viscous fan clutch (viscous coupling) for the engine cooling fan on the OM471 MP4 / MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros. Signs it has failed: the engine runs hot on hills or in traffic, the fan roars all the time, or oil is leaking from the hub. Replaces OE A 470 200 07 22, same as MAHLE CFC 173 000P, NRF 49159 and Nissens 86140.
