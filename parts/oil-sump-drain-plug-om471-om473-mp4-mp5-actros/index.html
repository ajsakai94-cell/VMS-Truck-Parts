Plastic oil sump drain plug to suit the OM471 and OM473 engines in the MP4 / MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros, OE A 001 990 75 17 (also fitted to Arocs, Antos and the Detroit DD13 / DD15 family). It's the black wing-head plug with the green O-ring that sits in the bottom of the plastic sump. These crack or get chewed up from being over-tightened or hit with a rattle gun, and once the O-ring goes hard it'll weep oil. Worth keeping a spare for oil changes. Same part as febi 100989, Trucktec 01.67.544 and DT 4.91215. Hand tight plus a quarter turn, never an impact gun. Aftermarket, not genuine Mercedes. Not sure it suits your truck? Send us your VIN and we'll check.

At a glance:

  • Position: Bottom of the plastic oil sump (oil pan)
  • Type: Plastic wing-head drain plug with valve tip and O-ring
  • Length: approx. 38 mm
  • In the box: 1 plug with O-ring fitted
  • Engines: OM470, OM471, OM473
  • Trucks: MP4 / MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros, Arocs, Antos
