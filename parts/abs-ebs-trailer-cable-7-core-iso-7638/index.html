ABS/EBS 7 core trailer plug cable for trailer stability control. A coiled suzi with ISO 7638 7-pin plugs at both ends that carries power and data between the prime mover and the trailer's ABS/EBS brake module (Wabco, Knorr-Bremse or Haldex), including roll stability control. Suits the MP4 Mercedes-Benz Actros and any prime mover and trailer with ISO 7638 sockets, and replaces Narva, Hella and Trail Link 7 core EBS suzi coils. Replace it if the trailer ABS/EBS warning light comes on, the plugs are loose or cracked, or the coil is cut or flattened.

At a glance:

  • Plugs: ISO 7638 7-pin ABS/EBS, both ends
  • Cores: 7 core
  • Voltage: 24V
  • Use: Trailer ABS/EBS brakes and roll stability control
  • Type: Coiled suzi with spring-latched plugs and dust caps
