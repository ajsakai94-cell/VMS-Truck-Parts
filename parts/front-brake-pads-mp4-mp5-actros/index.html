Front Brake Pad Set (Axle Set) to suit Mercedes-Benz Actros MP4 / MP5

Front axle brake pad set for the MP4 Mercedes-Benz Actros with Knorr-Bremse SM7 disc brakes. 4 pads plus a repair kit, enough to do both front wheels. Front and rear pads are different sizes, so check the WVA number on your old pad (29246 or 29247) before you order, or send us your VIN and we'll confirm fit. If your old pads say WVA 29315 you have Wabco MAXX22L calipers and these won't fit. New stock is on its way.

At a glance:

  • Axle: Front
  • Caliper: Knorr-Bremse SM7
  • WVA: 29246 / 29247
  • Pad size: 244 x 115 x 30 mm
  • In the box: 4 pads plus repair kit, enough for one front axle (both wheels)
  • Material: Carbon fibre
