Aftermarket exhaust manifold gasket to suit the OM473 15.6 litre engine in the MP4 / MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros 2658 and 2663. Replace it if you hear an exhaust tick or blow at the manifold, see soot marks around the manifold joints, or whenever the manifold or turbo comes off. There are two: A 472 142 18 80 for cylinders 1-3 and A 472 142 19 80 for cylinders 4-6. Pick the position you need, and add one of each to do the whole engine. Priced per gasket.

At a glance:

  • Positions: A 472 142 18 80 = cylinders 1-3, A 472 142 19 80 = cylinders 4-6
  • Engine: OM473 15.6 L (Actros 2658, 2663)
  • Fits between: Exhaust manifold and cylinder head
