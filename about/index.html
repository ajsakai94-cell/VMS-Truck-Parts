About VMS Truck Parts

VMS Truck Parts is an independent online parts supplier in Melbourne, Victoria that specialises in parts for the MP4 and MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros (OM471 and OM473 engines). Every part is listed by Mercedes-Benz OE number with aftermarket cross-references, the shop checks fitment from your VIN or rego before you pay, and orders ship Australia-wide by Australia Post or can be picked up free in Victoria.

What we do

  • One truck, done properly. We sell parts for the MP4 and MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros only, with the OM471 (12.8 litre) and OM473 (15.6 litre) engines. That's 38 parts across filters, cooling, clutch, engine, brakes, cab, workshop, tools.
  • Listed by part number. Every part shows its Mercedes-Benz OE number, written the ways mechanics type it, plus MAHLE, MANN, Hengst, WVA, Nissens and other aftermarket equivalents. See every MP4 / MP5 part number in one table.
  • Fitment checked before you pay. Text your VIN or rego and a photo of the old part to 0481 309 893 and we'll confirm the right part.
  • Postage at cost. Filters and small parts go in Australia Post satchels from $11.70, anywhere in Australia. Bigger parts are priced live to your postcode in the cart. Pickup in Victoria is free.
  • Easy returns. Unused parts can be returned within 30 days. See returns.

Popular parts

Service filters for the OM471 and OM473 (oil, fuel, water separator, air, cabin, AdBlue), the 5-filter service kit, brake pads, the viscous fan clutch, starter motor, oil spinner, battery terminal sensor, mirror covers and trailer suzi coils. 27 of our 39 listings are in stock right now.
