Actros MP4 / MP5 Brake Pad Part Numbers: Front and Rear

The front and rear brake pads on the MP4 and MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros are different sizes, so you need the right number for each axle. Most MP4s run Knorr-Bremse disc brakes: an SM7 caliper on the front and an SL7 on the rear.

Other numbers that turn up for the same pads: front A 006 420 52 20 and A 000 421 72 10; rear A 006 420 15 20. Treat these as "check the old pad" rather than a sure match.

Check your caliper first

Some MP4s run a Wabco MAXX22L caliper instead of Knorr. Those use a different pad (WVA 29315, front and rear). The WVA number is stamped on the backing plate of the old pad, so match it before you order.

Quick answers

Are the front and rear brake pads the same on an Actros MP4?

No. Front pads are OE A 006 420 14 20 (WVA 29246 / 29247, Knorr SM7 caliper) and rear pads are OE A 006 420 53 20 (WVA 29244 / 29245, Knorr SL7 caliper).

What if my MP4 has Wabco calipers?

Some MP4s run a Wabco MAXX22L caliper, which uses a different pad (WVA 29315). Match the WVA number stamped on the backing plate of your old pad.
