Actros MP4 Service Intervals and Filter Checklist

The MP4 Actros doesn't run on one fixed service interval. Its maintenance system works out when each service is due from how the truck is used: the load, the kilometres, idle time, the fuel and the oil specification. It shows the result on the instrument cluster. Two MP4s doing different work can be due at very different kilometres, so always go by your truck's maintenance display and service records rather than a number from the internet.

What doesn't change much is what gets replaced. Here's the filter checklist for an OM471 or OM473 service, with the OE numbers so you can match the parts.

Tips for Australian conditions

  • Dust is the big one. On gravel or in dry country, check the air filter restriction indicator every few days rather than waiting for the service.
  • Water in the fuel shows up fast on the MP4's water separator. If the warning comes on, drain the bowl, and change the pre-filter if it keeps happening.
  • An AdBlue warning with power loss is often a blocked AdBlue pump filter, which is a cheap part to try first.
  • Keep the old filter until the new one is fitted, so you can compare the number printed on it.
