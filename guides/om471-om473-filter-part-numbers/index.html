OM471 / OM473 Filter Part Numbers for the Actros MP4 / MP5

These are the filters for the MP4 and MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros with the OM471 and OM473 engines. The OE number is printed on the old filter or its box, and looks like A 472 184 04 25. When you search this site you can type it with or without the spaces and the leading A.

Where one part has several OE numbers, they're superseded or alternative numbers for the same filter. Cross-references are the equivalent MAHLE, MANN, Hengst and other aftermarket numbers.
