Actros MP4 AdBlue Warning or Derate: What to Check First

The MP4 and MP5 Actros use AdBlue (urea, also called DEF) in the SCR system to cut NOx from the exhaust. When the system sees a fault it warns you on the dash, and if the fault isn't fixed the truck goes into derate (limp mode). That's why AdBlue problems feel urgent.

A warning can come from a simple supply problem or from a failed sensor. Start with the cheap checks below before paying for a diagnosis.

1. AdBlue level and quality

Check the tank isn't low and that it was filled with AdBlue, not water or diesel. Old or contaminated AdBlue causes faults too. If anything other than AdBlue went in, stop and have the tank drained and flushed. A missing or loose cap lets dirt and water in, so replace a lost one. Our Locking AdBlue Tank Cap with 2 Keys locks with a key, which also stops anyone topping it up with the wrong thing.

2. The AdBlue pump filter

The AdBlue pump has its own filter. When it blocks, the pump can't supply enough AdBlue to the doser, which brings up a warning. It's a cheap part, so it's worth ruling out early.

3. White crystals around the lines

AdBlue dries to white crystals. Crusting around the pump, the lines or the dosing injector on the exhaust usually means a leak at that spot.

4. When it's a workshop job

If the supply side checks out, the fault is usually further along. Repair shops see AdBlue pump and dosing faults, NOx sensors, DPF regeneration problems and sticking EGR valves on Euro 6 trucks of this age. These need the fault codes read with a diagnostic tool, so take it to a workshop rather than swapping parts on a guess.

Quick answers

What is the AdBlue filter part number for the Actros MP4?

OE A 000 142 05 89. MAHLE lists MAHLE UX 13KIT for MP4 / MP5 trucks. Check the number on your old filter, or send us your VIN.

Can a blocked AdBlue filter cause derate?

Yes. A blocked filter starves the AdBlue dosing pump, which can bring up an emissions warning and put the truck into derate (limp mode). It's a cheap part, so it's a sensible thing to rule out early.

How often should the AdBlue filter be changed?

As the truck's maintenance system shows, with the AdBlue system service, or sooner if you get an AdBlue or emissions warning.

What if water or diesel got into the AdBlue tank?

Don't keep running it. Contaminated AdBlue can damage the pump and the SCR system, so have the tank drained and flushed by a workshop. A locking AdBlue cap stops it happening again.
