How to Find the Right Part Number for Your Actros MP4 / MP5

The quickest way to get the right part is the number printed on the old one. If you can't read it, your rego or VIN lets us look the truck up for you. Here's where to find each.

1. The number on the old part

Mercedes-Benz part numbers (OE numbers) start with an A and look like A 472 184 04 25. They're printed or stamped on the part, on a sticker, or on the box. Aftermarket parts carry their own brand's number, such as MAHLE OX 832D or MANN HU 12 001 z.

Type either kind into the search box. Spaces and dashes don't matter, and you can leave off the A.

2. Which engine you have

Every truck part on this site suits the MP4 and MP5 Actros. A few engine parts depend on the engine. The model number on the cab, such as 2653, gives you the power: the last two digits are the horsepower divided by ten.

The engine type is also on the plate on the engine block and on your service records. Pick your engine on the home page and we'll only show parts that suit it.

3. Your VIN or rego

The VIN (Mercedes calls it the FIN) is 17 characters and starts with W. It's on your registration papers and on the vehicle data plate in the door frame. Your rego and state are usually enough for us to look it up.
