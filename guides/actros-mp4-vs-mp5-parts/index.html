Actros MP4 vs MP5: Do the Same Parts Fit Both?

The MP4 Actros went on sale in Australia around late 2016 to early 2017. The MP5 replaced it from 2020. Both use the same OM471 and OM473 engines, so most engine, filter, cooling, brake and driveline parts are the same. The differences are mostly in the cab: switches, lights and some body panels.

Of the 38 truck parts on this site, 35 are listed for both the MP4 and MP5. The rest are listed for the MP4 only.

Parts that fit both

Filters, brake pads, cooling parts, the clutch, the starter and most engine parts suit both. The full part number table shows what each part suits, and every part page says it in the "Fits" line.

How to check before you order

  • Match the number on the old part. Mercedes OE numbers start with A, like A 960 545 12 13. Type it into the search box.
  • Check the build date on the rego papers or the data plate in the door frame. A truck first registered in 2020 or 2021 could be either model.
  • Send us your VIN or rego. Text it to 0481 309 893 and we'll confirm the right part before you pay.

Quick answers

Do MP4 filters fit the MP5 Actros?

Our filters are listed for both the MP4 and the MP5, because both use the OM471 and OM473 engines. Match the OE number on your old filter to be sure.

What years is the MP4 Actros in Australia?

Roughly late 2016 or early 2017 to 2020, when the MP5 replaced it. Build dates vary, so the date on your rego papers or the data plate is a better guide than the year it was first registered.

Which parts are different between the MP4 and MP5?

Mostly cab, body and electrical parts. On this site the H7 headlight globes, the driver's door switch panel and the big-cab exterior sun visor are listed for the MP4 only. Each part page says which trucks it suits.
