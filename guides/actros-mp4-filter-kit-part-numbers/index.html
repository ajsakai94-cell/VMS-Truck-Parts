Actros MP4 Service Filter Kit Part Numbers (OM471 / OM473)

A standard oil service on the MP4 or MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros (OM471 or OM473) uses five filters: engine oil, the main fuel filter, the fuel pre-filter / water separator, the engine air filter and the cabin filter. Per the MAHLE MP4 / MP5 catalogue, the oil, fuel, pre-filter and air filters are the same parts on the OM471 and the OM473.

Mercedes numbers are printed with spaces, like A 472 184 04 25, but invoices and parts systems often run them together as A4721840425 or drop the A. All of those are the same part, and they're all listed below.

Numbers to double-check

  • Air filter: MAHLE also lists LX 5438 (OE A 004 094 97 04) for the same trucks, which looks like a different housing size. Match the number on your old element.
  • Fuel filter: some catalogues list A 471 090 24 55 or 08 55 instead of 27 55. Check the number on your old filter or its box.
  • Cabin filter: MP4 / MP5 cabs use more than one cabin filter, including a carbon version (A 960 830 01 18, MAHLE LAK 863). Ours is the pollen filter, A 960 830 02 18.
