Actros MP4 Losing Coolant? What to Check First

Coolant loss is one of the most common problems on an MP4 Actros at 6 to 10 years old. Start with the cheap outside checks and work in. Never open the expansion tank cap while the engine is hot.

The hidden one: the EGR cooler

Repair shops report that when an MP4 loses coolant with no visible leak, the EGR cooler cracking inside is the most common cause. The coolant goes into the exhaust side, so you won't see a drip. A workshop can confirm it with a cooling system pressure test before you spend money on parts. Rule out the tank, cap and hoses first, because they're much cheaper.

Running hot without losing coolant

If the level holds but the temperature climbs on hills or in traffic, check the Viscous Fan Clutch. A fan that roars all the time, or oil leaking from the hub, are also signs it has failed. A blocked radiator core does the same thing.

Doing the job once

If the radiator is coming out, it's worth replacing the top hose and checking the expansion tank and cap at the same time. The engine and cooling part numbers guide lists every OE number we stock. Not sure what you need? Text a photo and your VIN to 0481 309 893.

Quick answers

Why is my Actros losing coolant with no visible leak?

On the MP4 the most common cause is an EGR cooler cracking inside, which lets coolant into the exhaust side where you can't see it. A workshop can pressure test the cooling system to confirm it. Check the cheaper outside causes first: the expansion tank, its cap and the hoses.

Can a cracked expansion tank cause coolant loss?

Yes. The plastic expansion tank cracks with age and heat. A sweet smell, crusty dried coolant around the tank, or a slow drop in the level are the usual signs. Replace the cap at the same time if it doesn't hold pressure.

What is the part number for the MP4 expansion tank?

OE A 960 501 42 03, also A 960 501 87 03. Aftermarket equivalents are MAHLE CRT 235 000P and Nissens 996053.

My Actros runs hot but isn't losing coolant. What should I check?

Look at the viscous fan clutch. If the engine runs hot on hills or in traffic, or the fan roars all the time, or oil is leaking from the hub, the fan clutch has probably failed. A blocked radiator core is the other common cause.
