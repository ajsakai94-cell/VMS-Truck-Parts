ABS/EBS vs 7 Pin Trailer Suzi Coil: Which One Do You Need?

A prime mover and trailer are joined by the air lines plus two electrical suzi coils. One carries the trailer lights. The other, the ABS/EBS cable, carries power and data to the trailer's brake module. They look similar, but they do different jobs and the plugs are different.

Signs it needs replacing

  • ABS/EBS cable: the trailer ABS/EBS warning light comes on, the plugs are loose or cracked, or the coil is cut or flattened.
  • Light suzi: a trailer light flickers or stops working, a pin is burnt or corroded, or the cable has kinked at the plug.

Check the plug before you order

ISO 7638 ABS/EBS plugs are a standard, so the ABS/EBS cable suits any truck and trailer with those sockets. Australian trailers use more than one style of 7 pin lighting plug, so check the plug on your truck and trailer matches the photo on the part page. Not sure? Quick answers

What's the difference between an EBS suzi coil and a normal suzi coil?

An ABS/EBS suzi (ISO 7638) carries power and data to the trailer's ABS/EBS brake module, including roll stability control. A normal 7 pin suzi carries the trailer lights: tail, stop, indicators and auxiliary. They're not interchangeable.

Can I use a 7 pin light suzi for the trailer EBS?

No. The trailer brakes need the ISO 7638 ABS/EBS cable. The light suzi only carries the lighting circuits.

Does the ABS/EBS suzi coil fit any truck?

It suits any prime mover and trailer with ISO 7638 sockets, including the MP4 Actros. ISO 7638 is a standard plug, so the make of the truck or trailer brake system (Wabco, Knorr-Bremse or Haldex) doesn't matter.

When should I replace a suzi coil?

If the trailer ABS/EBS warning light comes on, a light on the trailer stops working, the plugs are loose or cracked, or the coil is cut, flattened or has lost its spring.
