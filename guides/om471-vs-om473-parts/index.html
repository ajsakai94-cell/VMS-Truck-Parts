OM471 vs OM473: Which Actros MP4 / MP5 Parts Are Different?

The MP4 and MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros sold in Australia came with one of two six-cylinder engines: the OM471 (12.8 litre) or the bigger OM473 (15.6 litre). Most service parts are the same on both. The ones that differ are mostly the big engine parts: the head, the liners, the turbo and the exhaust manifold.

Which engine have you got?

The model number on the cab gives you the power: the last two digits are the horsepower divided by ten.

Model on the cabPowerEngine (usually)
2643, 2646430 to 460 hpOM471 (12.8 litre)
2653530 hpOM471 (12.8 litre)
2658, 2663580 to 630 hpOM473 (15.6 litre)

That's the usual pattern, not a guarantee. The engine type is also on the plate on the engine block and in your service records. If you're not sure, the part number guide shows where to look, or text your VIN to 0481 309 893.

Quick answers

Do the OM471 and OM473 use the same filters?

Mostly, yes. Per the MAHLE MP4 / MP5 catalogue, the oil, fuel, pre-filter and air filters are the same parts on both engines, and our service filter kit suits both. The oil filter also comes in a newer slim-base style (A 473 180 05 09), so match the number on your old filter.

How do I tell if my Actros has an OM471 or an OM473?

The model number on the cab is the quickest guide: 2643, 2646 and 2653 usually have the 12.8 litre OM471, and 2658 and 2663 usually have the 15.6 litre OM473. The engine type is also on the plate on the engine block and on your service records. Or text your VIN or rego to 0481 309 893 and we'll check.

Is the OM471 the same as the Detroit DD15?

They come from the same engine family (and the OM473 matches the DD16), so some parts are shared. Don't assume a part swaps across unless its page says so: our OM473 cylinder head is one that is listed for the DD15 / DD16 as well.
