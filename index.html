FAQ

Will this part fit my truck?

Every truck part on this site is for the MP4 and MP5 Mercedes-Benz Actros. If you're unsure about your build, call or text 0481 309 893 with your VIN and we'll confirm before you order.

How much is shipping?

$19.95 anywhere in Australia, and free on orders over $250. Heavy or bulky items such as clutch kits may cost more, and we'll contact you before sending.

Yes. Choose "Pickup" at checkout and we'll message you when your order is ready.

Usually, yes. Tap "Ask about this part" or call us and we'll give you a price and lead time.
